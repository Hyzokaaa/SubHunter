Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Data Collection

SubHunter does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The application runs entirely on your device.

2. Subtitle Providers

When searching for subtitles, SubHunter sends video file hashes and metadata (series name, season, episode) to third-party subtitle providers (OpenSubtitles, Addic7ed, etc.). These providers have their own privacy policies. No personal information is sent.

3. Configuration

Settings and credentials (if you add OpenSubtitles account details) are stored locally on your device in ~/.subhunter/config.json. They are never transmitted to us.

4. Update Checks

SubHunter checks the GitHub Releases API to notify you of new versions. This sends a standard HTTP request to GitHub's servers. No personal data is included.

5. Donations

Donations are processed by Paddle. When you donate, Paddle collects payment information according to their privacy policy. We do not have access to your payment details.

6. Logs

Activity logs are stored locally at ~/.subhunter/logs/subhunter.log. They contain only subtitle search results and error messages. Logs are never uploaded or shared.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, open a ticket at GitHub Issues.
